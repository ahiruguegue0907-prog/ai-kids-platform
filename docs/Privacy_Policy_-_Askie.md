# Privacy Policy

Last Updated: February 2026

## Introduction

Askie ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting the privacy of children and their families. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information when you use our AI-powered educational platform.

## COPPA Compliance

Askie is fully compliant with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 without verifiable parental consent.

Parents have full control over their child's information and can review, modify, or delete it at any time through the parent dashboard.

## Information We Collect

### Parent Account Information

Email address

Name

Subscription and usage information

### Child Profile Information

Child's first name (optional nickname)

Age or date of birth

Avatar selection

Learning preferences and interests

### Usage Information

Conversation history (text and voice)

Generated images

Usage statistics (queries, time spent, features used)

Device information and IP address

## How We Use Information

We use collected information to:

Provide personalized, age-appropriate educational content

Maintain conversation history for parental review

Improve our AI models and safety features

Process subscriptions and payments

Send important updates about the service (with parental consent)

Ensure child safety through content moderation

## AI Services and Data Processing

Askie uses Google Gemini to provide educational content, including text responses, educational images, and voice conversations. Google Gemini processes requests in real time but does NOT store user queries or responses.

The app requests explicit in-app consent before any data is sent to Google Gemini. Users can decline and no data will be transmitted.

Google Gemini maintains content safety filters to ensure all generated content is appropriate for children.

## Information Sharing

We do NOT sell, rent, or share your child's personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

We may share information only in these limited circumstances:

With Google Gemini for real-time AI processing of your child's questions — Google Gemini does NOT store queries or responses

With service providers who help us operate the platform (e.g., cloud hosting, payment processing) under strict confidentiality agreements

To comply with legal obligations or protect rights and safety

With your explicit consent

## Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures:

End-to-end encryption for all data transmission

Secure, encrypted storage of all personal information

Regular security audits and updates

Strict access controls and authentication

Secure payment processing through certified providers

## Parental Rights

Parents have the right to:

Review all information collected about their child

Request deletion of their child's information

Refuse further collection or use of their child's information

Access and download conversation history

Set usage limits and parental controls

To exercise these rights, contact us at [askie@kidsai.app](mailto:askie@kidsai.app)

## Data Retention

We retain conversation history and usage data for as long as your account is active. When you delete a child profile or close your account, we permanently delete all associated data within 30 days, except where required by law.

To prevent repeat abuse of free access, we retain anonymized account identifiers (hashed Auth0 user IDs) for up to 12 months after account deletion.

## Cookies and Tracking

### Essential Cookies

We use essential cookies to maintain your session and provide the service. These cookies are necessary for the platform to function and cannot be disabled.

### Analytics Cookies (Optional)

With your consent, we use Facebook Pixel to understand how visitors use our website and to improve our service. Facebook Pixel collects:

Page views and navigation patterns

General usage statistics (registration, subscription interest)

Device and browser information

We do NOT share personally identifiable information about children with Facebook or any third-party analytics provider. Analytics are used only to understand aggregate usage patterns of parents and visitors.

You can accept or decline analytics cookies through our cookie consent banner, which appears on your first visit. You can change your preference at any time by clearing your browser's local storage and refreshing the page. You can also control cookie settings through your browser preferences.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. We will notify parents of any material changes via email and update the "Last Updated" date. Continued use of the service after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

## Contact Us

If you have questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy or our data practices, please contact us:

Email: [askie@kidsai.app](mailto:askie@kidsai.app)
