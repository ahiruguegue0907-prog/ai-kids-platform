## Age-Specific Safety Considerations

Ages 3-5: Early Learners

✅ Recommended Guidelines

Always supervise AI interactions

Limit sessions to 15-20 minutes

Focus on simple, concrete topics

Review all generated content together

Emphasize creative and educational use

⚠ Watch For

Requests for personal information

Complex or confusing responses

Inappropriate content attempts

Over-reliance on AI answers

Ages 6-8: Growing Explorers

✅ Recommended Guidelines

Check in during sessions periodically

Review conversation history weekly

Teach basic digital citizenship

Encourage questions about AI responses

Balance AI with traditional learning

⚠ Watch For

Sharing personal details

Attempting to bypass filters

Excessive screen time

Believing all AI responses without question

Ages 9-12: Independent Users

✅ Recommended Guidelines

Review monthly usage patterns

Discuss AI ethics and limitations

Teach critical evaluation of AI content

Establish clear usage boundaries

Encourage responsible AI citizenship

⚠ Watch For

Inappropriate content generation attempts

Sharing sensitive family information

Dependence on AI for schoolwork

Attempts to exploit AI vulnerabilities

## Free AI Safety Checklist for Parents

Use this checklist to ensure your child's AI experience is safe. Check off each item as you go.

### Before Your Child Uses AI

[-]

Review the app's privacy policy and COPPA compliance [-] Set up parental controls and content filters [-] Create an age-appropriate child profile [-] Establish clear rules for AI usage time [-] Discuss what personal information should never be shared

### During AI Sessions

[-]

Supervise younger children (under 8) during sessions [-] Check that responses are age-appropriate [-] Encourage your child to ask questions about AI answers [-] Watch for attempts to share personal details [-] Ensure a healthy balance with non-screen activities

### Ongoing Monitoring

[-]

Review conversation history weekly [-] Check usage time reports monthly [-] Discuss what your child learned from AI [-] Update content filters as your child grows [-] Teach critical thinking about AI-generated content
